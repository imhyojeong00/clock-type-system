Clock-Type System

Succulents arose in many places at once and survived by grafting and shedding parts. This site carries that logic over to clocks.

Each letter from A to Y is a clock. Type one and it is rebuilt on a single shared arbor — the axle the hands turn on — with the case, numerals, hands and second hand of five other clocks grafted onto it.

  1. Type A–Y. Clocks line up like a sentence and overlap. Z is still empty.
  2. Type the same letter twice and the hands fall out.
  3. Swipe to look back along the line, tap a letter to jump to its clock, pinch to zoom.
  4. Turn on X-RAY to watch each clock being assembled from the inside. With sound on, every clock keeps ticking in its own rhythm.

Im Hyojeong, Advanced Course, Kaywon University of Art & Design, Korea
